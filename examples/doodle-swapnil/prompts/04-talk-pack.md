# Talk pack — Doodle Swapnil

Style bible (repeat exactly in every generation): soft Korean doodle, bold black outlines, big round eyes, pink tulip accent, hot pink + sunny yellow palette. Palette: #ff4d8d, #ffd23f.
Character: Doodle Swapnil, wearing doctor's scrubs.
Ground: solid chroma green (#00FF00) background, no text, no watermark, no drop shadow, nothing touching the edges.
Reference: the model sheet. Canvas 720x936, character centred on one floor line, same scale in every image. ONE generation per image:

1. base — neutral standing pose, mouth closed (this is the canvas all patches sit on)
2. pose wave — waving hello
3. pose point — pointing at the viewer / at an idea
4. pose open — both palms open, "why / what" shrug energy
5. pose thumbs — thumbs up

Then mark the mouth rect (A/O/E) and the blink rect on the base in rigpop-talk, or measure them from the face kit, and fill them into character.rigpop.json. `rigpop-forge qa` checks every patch image against its rect.
