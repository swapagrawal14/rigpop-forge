# Pose packs — Mocha the Cat

Style bible (repeat exactly in every generation): chubby orange cat in a teal hoodie, bold black outlines, flat colours, cream background energy.
Character: Mocha the Cat.
Ground: solid chroma green (#00FF00) background, no text, no watermark, no drop shadow, nothing touching the edges.
Reference: the model sheet.

For any action a puppet cannot sell (walk, jump, laugh, cook, dance): paint 4-8 FULL-BODY key poses of the action, ONE pose per generation, same scale, feet on one floor line. Name the pack after the action (`poses/<action>/key0.png`, `key1.png`, ...).

Locomotion = flip-book, conversation = cut-out. That rule is the whole engine.
